Welcome to Mr. & Ms. Technovation!

Welcome to the Mr. and Ms. Technovation event, a platform to showcase your technical, managerial, and creative skills. This competition is designed to test your knowledge, problem-solving abilities, and innovative thinking across multiple rounds.

Event Details

  • Date: 4th January 2025
  • Time: 01:30 PM - 03:30 PM
  • Venue: Auditorium
  • Registration Fee: ₹100 per person
  • Team Size: 1 participant

Prizes

  • 1st Place: Gifts & Certificate

What Will Happen?

  • Showcase your technical, managerial, and creative skills in three challenging rounds: Round 1: Technical Quiz & Snippet Challenge – Test your knowledge with code snippets and MCQs. Round 2: Visual Coding Challenge – Replicate designs through coding. Round 3: Project Management Challenge – Plan and present a complete project lifecycle

Rules & Guidelines

*Wearing the college ID is mandatory for all participants during the event.
  • Each participant will compete individually.
  • Male participants must wear formal attire.
  • Female participants must wear simple formal suits.
  • Participants must strictly adhere to the time limits set for each round.
  • Participants are expected to maintain professional behavior and decorum throughout the event.
  • Only authorized materials, such as pens and notebooks, are allowed.
  • Electronic devices may only be used if explicitly permitted for a specific round.
  • All decisions made by the judging panel will be final and binding in all rounds.
  • Participants failing to comply with the rules or unable to complete tasks within the allotted time may be disqualified.
  • Latecomers will not be permitted to join once a round has commenced.
  • All work produced during the event must be original and completed solely by the participant.
